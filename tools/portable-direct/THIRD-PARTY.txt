The direct-source portable bundle includes:

OpenJDK 25 / Eclipse Temurin (https://adoptium.net/). Module-specific legal
notices remain in the extracted support/runtime/legal directory.

OpenJFX 25 (https://openjfx.io/). License and third-party notices are under
support/licenses/javafx. Native libraries remain inside the original Maven JARs.

Gson 2.13.2 (https://github.com/google/gson), Apache License 2.0. See
support/licenses/gson-LICENSE.

JLayer 1.0.1 (https://sourceforge.net/projects/javalayer/), LGPL 2.1 or later.
The replaceable support/app/jlayer.jar is distributed with corresponding source
under support/licenses/jlayer-1.0.1-sources.jar.

7zr 26.05 (https://www.7-zip.org/download.html) identifies itself as public
domain. It only extracts the bundled support/platform.7z on first launch.

Game artwork and music credits remain in the project source documentation.
